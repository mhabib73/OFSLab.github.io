Rodrigo Amezcua-Correa is a Professor at the College of Optics and Photonics
(CREOL), University of Central Florida. He obtained his PhD degree from the
Optoelectronics Research Centre (ORC) at the University of Southampton. His
Ph.D. was on the development of hollow core fibers. After completing his doctoral
research, Rodrigo was as a post-doctoral researcher with Prof. Jonathan
Knight at the University of Bath, UK. At the University of Bath, he developed
hollow core fiber fabrication methods that led to fibers with significantly
improved optical properties. He has extensive experience in the design and
fabrication of optical fiber and photonic technologies for several applications;
including communications, fiber lasers, light sources and sensing systems. Before
joining CREOL, he worked at Powerlase Photonics, fabricating high-power
diode pumped solid-state lasers. He is the co-founder and Chief Technology
Officer at Relativity Networks commercializing hollow-core fiber technology
and a fellow of Optica.
